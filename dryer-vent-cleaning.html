WHY IT MATTERS

A small clog can become a big problem

Even if you clean the lint screen, fine fibers still make their way into the vent line. Over time, airflow gets restricted, the dryer runs hotter, and loads take longer. Cleaning the vent helps the dryer run smoother and helps lower overheating risk.

Shorter cycles

Dry clothes faster with better airflow.

Less wasted energy

Reduce extra run-time over the month.

Lower strain can help extend lifespan.

SIGNS

Signs your vent needs attention

Dryer vents don’t always fail loudly—often it shows up as slower drying and more heat. If you’re noticing any of these, it’s a good time to schedule.

Multiple drying cycles

Airflow may be restricted inside the vent line.

Extra heat in the laundry room

Trapped warm air can mean a clogged vent path.

Weak airflow outside

The outside flap barely opens during use.

Hot or musty smell

Lint and moisture can create unpleasant odor during cycles.

HOW WE WORK

STEP 2
