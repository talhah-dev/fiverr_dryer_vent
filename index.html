WARNING SIGNS

When airflow drops, your dryer works harder and runs hotter.

Lint and dust can build up deep inside the vent line where you can’t see it. If you notice any of these signs, it’s a good time to schedule a professional cleaning.

Clothes take longer to dry

If you’re running extra cycles, the vent may be restricting exhaust.

Laundry room feels too hot

Excess heat usually means air isn’t escaping like it should.

Burning or musty smell

Lint can overheat or moisture can linger when airflow is blocked.

Weak outside airflow

If the vent flap barely opens, lint buildup may be blocking the line.
