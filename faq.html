WARNING SIGNS

Signs it’s time to schedule

If you notice one of these, your dryer may be working harder than it should.

Clothes take longer to dry

Multiple cycles often means restricted airflow.

Laundry room feels extra hot

Heat has nowhere to go when the vent is blocked.

Weak airflow outside

The outside flap barely moves during a cycle.

Unusual smell during drying

A hot or musty smell can signal trapped heat or moisture.

WHY IT MATTERS

Airflow affects safety and performance

When lint builds up in the vent line, airflow drops. The dryer runs hotter, takes longer, and may struggle to push out moisture. Keeping the vent clear supports smoother operation and helps reduce overheating risk.

Less strain on the dryer

Better airflow can mean fewer cycles.

Lower overheating risk

Lint buildup is a common safety concern.

TIMING

How often should you clean it?

Frequency depends on household use, vent length, and how much lint your dryer produces.

Most households

A regular schedule helps prevent buildup from becoming a problem.

Heavy use homes

Large families and frequent loads may benefit from more frequent checks.

Long vent runs

Longer duct routes can collect lint faster and restrict airflow sooner.

Pets at home

Pet hair can increase lint buildup and slow down drying.

Best approach

If you’ve noticed any warning signs, it’s smart to schedule soon rather than waiting.

PREVENTION

Easy ways to keep airflow strong

A few simple habits can help reduce lint buildup between cleanings.

Clean the lint screen

Every load helps keep airflow consistent.

Check the outside vent

The flap should open well during a cycle.

Avoid crushed vent lines

Make sure the dryer has room behind it.

Don’t ignore warning signs

Long dry times are a signal to check airflow.

Pro tip

If your dryer suddenly starts taking longer, don’t just increase heat—check airflow first.
